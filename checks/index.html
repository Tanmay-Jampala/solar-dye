QM9 (Ramakrishnan et al., Sci. Data 1, 140022, 2014; CC0) was obtained from the DeepChem/MoleculeNet mirror (https://deepchemdata.s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/datasets/qm9.csv) together with the authors' list of geometrically inconsistent ('uncharacterized') molecules from figshare, on 2026-11-11. It contained 133885 molecules; removing the uncharacterized set and any SMILES failing RDKit sanitisation left 130831. HOMO, LUMO and gap are B3LYP/6-31G(2df,p) values, converted from Hartree to eV. HOPV15 (Lopez et al., Sci. Data 3, 160086, 2016; CC BY 4.0) revision 2 was obtained from figshare (https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/4513735) on the same date: 350 molecules parsed, 350 retained after RDKit sanitisation. Its HOMO/LUMO/gap columns are experimental (electrochemical) values in eV with PCE in percent; B3LYP/def2-SVP orbital energies are averaged over the supplied conformers. The AM1.5G spectrum is the ASTM G173-03 table as redistributed with pvlib-python v0.11.0.
