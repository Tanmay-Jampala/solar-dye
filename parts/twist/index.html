What we did

Bithiophene, the simplest two-ring dye backbone, twisted about its S-C-C-S dihedral in steps from 0 ° to 180 °. At every angle everything else relaxed (B3LYP/6-31G*, relaxed scan (all other coordinates optimised)). The whole scan took 1.2 h on the laptop.

What we found

The lowest energy is at 160 °, slightly twisted. Flattening it costs only 3.9 meV, which is 0.15 kT: at room temperature the molecule flexes freely through planar.

Twisting it to 90 ° costs 0.118 eV and breaks the conjugation between the rings: the gap opens by 1.605 eV.
