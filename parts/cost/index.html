energy = CPU TDP x measured wall time (upper-bound estimate, not a measurement).

What we did

Every quantum calculation was timed: 40 of them. Energy is the processor's rated power, 45 W, times the measured time; cost and CO₂ follow from the Karnataka tariff and India's grid emission factor.

What we found

The whole project used 0.198 kWh and cost ₹1.15. Running B3LYP on every QM9 molecule instead would take 1.33 years and 525 kWh. The trained model screens all of QM9 in 2.68 s.

Logarithmic scale, one tick per factor of ten. CPU energy per operation is the rated power divided by the peak rate of 2.4 × 10¹¹ operations per second. Note: one floating-point operation erases more than one bit, so per-bit the real CPU is somewhat closer to the limit than this ratio says; the order of magnitude is the point.
