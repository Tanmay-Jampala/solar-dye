What is the best band gap a single-junction cell could have under real sunlight?

What we did

Detailed balance: every photon above the gap makes one electron, every photon below it is lost, and the cell itself glows like a body at 300 K. Integrating that against the measured AM1.5G spectrum, gap by gap in steps of 0.01 eV, gives the most power any single-junction cell can deliver.

What we found

The best gap is 1.34 eV, at 33.68 %. A finer search puts the peak at 1.336 eV. That agrees with the published value (Ruhle, Solar Energy 130 (2016) 139: 1.34 eV, 33.7 %).

The curve is not smooth: water vapour cuts a band out of real sunlight, which leaves a second peak at 1.15 eV (H2O absorption band near 1.13 um in AM1.5G).
